<h2 id="-features">✨ Features</h2>

### 🧠 Smart OCR — 50+ Languages

Supports Simplified & Traditional Chinese, Japanese, English, and major European languages (German, French, Spanish, Italian, etc.). Powered by PaddleOCR v6, running locally on your device's CPU/GPU (Apple Silicon Neural Engine acceleration on macOS). Accuracy rivals cloud-based services.

### 📐 Layout Analysis

Automatically preserves paragraphs, headings, tables, and images in their original reading order. Side-by-side comparison with high-fidelity Markdown output.

### 📊 Table Extraction

Automatically detects both bordered and borderless tables. Structured output ready for Excel editing. Complex multi-page tables merged and aligned with precision.

### 🔢 Formula Recognition

Math formulas automatically converted to LaTeX. Supports inline and block-level equations — integrals, matrices, chemical equations rendered flawlessly.

### 🔒 Privacy First

- ✅ All AI processing runs locally — your data never leaves your device
- ✅ Zero data collection, no ads, no analytics SDKs of any kind
- ✅ Purchase verification only via official stores (StoreKit / Windows Store) — no document data transmitted
