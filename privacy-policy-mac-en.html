Privacy Policy

Cloud Smart Doc Scan · 云帆智能文档扫描

Overview

Cloud Smart Doc Scan ("the Software") takes user privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the Software.

The Software is an offline desktop application — document scanning, OCR text recognition, layout analysis, table recognition, and formula recognition are all performed locally on your Mac. Your documents and images are never uploaded to any remote server.

Information We Collect

What We Do NOT Collect

  • Document Content: All documents, images, and PDFs are processed locally and never uploaded to any server.
  • Personal Information: We do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, or any other personal data.
  • Location Data: We do not access GPS or network-based location.
  • Other Files: We only access document/image files that you actively choose to import.

Data That May Be Generated

  • Crash Diagnostic Data: If the app crashes, macOS may generate a crash report stored locally on your device. These are not automatically sent to us. You can manage them in the Console app.
  • App Store License Verification: When installed via the Mac App Store, Apple verifies your purchase status. This is standard Apple platform behavior; we do not store or forward this data.

How We Use Data

  • Local OCR Inference: Document recognition runs locally on your Mac using built-in AI models (including Apple Silicon Neural Engine). No internet required.
  • Local Storage Only: Recognition results and exported files are saved only to your specified local paths. No cloud storage is used.
  • License Verification: Purchase status is verified solely through Apple's StoreKit framework. No document data is transmitted.

Network Usage

The Software accesses the network only for:

  • App Store License Verification: Validating purchase status and app receipts.
  • Purchase Operations: When you tap the Buy button, StoreKit processes the in-app purchase.

Core OCR, scanning, and recognition features all run offline without a network connection.

Third-Party Services

The Software does not integrate any third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or tracking services.

License verification uses Apple's built-in StoreKit framework. Data processing by StoreKit is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Data Security

All document processing is performed locally on your Mac. Your data never leaves your device, eliminating the risk of exposure during transmission or cloud storage. We recommend enabling macOS FileVault full-disk encryption for additional local protection.

Children's Privacy

This Software is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be distributed with new versions of the Software and posted on this page.
